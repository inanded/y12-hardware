Year 12 Computer Science · Hardware

Cover teacher notes

You do not need any Computer Science knowledge. Everything is on a website the pupils work through, with a class screen on the projector that keeps time. Your job is to start the clock, read out the red messages, and check that every pupil turns in their workbook.

Two lessons, 55 minutes each, same routine. Lesson 1: storage. Lesson 2: memory chips and embedded systems.

Before the lesson (2 minutes)

  1. Log in to the classroom PC and turn on the projector.
  2. Open the class screen: board.html?l=1 for Lesson 1, or ?l=2 for Lesson 2. It is also on the site's home page under "Teachers".
  3. Leave it on the projector, full screen if you can (F11).

When the class is in

Take the register as normal, then press Start lesson on the class screen and say:

"Open Teams and go to Assignments. Open today's Hardware assignment: there is a workbook and a link to the lesson website. Type your name in the workbook, then open the website and work through the stages in order. The board shows which stage you should be on. If you are stuck, use the help buttons on the site first."

During the lesson

  • The class screen shows the stage everyone should be on, and how long is left.
  • Red messages appear on the class screen at the key moments (exam questions, mark schemes, check-out, one minute left). Read each one out.
  • Content questions: say "Press Show me step by step on that stage. If you are still stuck, press I'm stuck and keep going." The site lists what they were stuck on, and they copy it into the workbook for their teacher.
  • Pupils may talk to the person next to them on the "write it" and "chip choice" stages, but both must write in their own workbook. The exam question stage is silent, with no notes.
  • If the class falls behind, that is fine. At the red message for exam questions, everyone moves on, finished or not.

If something goes wrong

  • The website will not load: pupils open the Offline Pack PDF attached to the same Teams assignment. It has every stage as pictures and text. The workbook works as normal.
  • A pupil has no device: they share with a neighbour; both names go on one workbook, and you note who.
  • Task Manager will not open (Lesson 2, stage 3): the site has a table of example laptops to use instead.

Before they leave

  1. At the one-minute message, every pupil shows you Turned in on Teams.
  2. Email the class teacher the names of anyone absent and anyone who did not turn in.
