Inside a DRAM cell

About 4 minutes · watch, then try it

Try it: switch the refresh off

Each square is one DRAM cell. Dark = charged = 1. The orange band is the memory controller refreshing one row at a time. Switch refresh off and watch what happens to the picture.

Refresh:1s lost: 0

Slowed right down. Real DRAM refreshes every row at least once every 64 ms.

Show me step by step

Think of each capacitor as a bucket with a small hole. A full bucket is a 1. Water leaks out all the time. Refresh means someone checks every bucket and tops up the full ones before they drop too low. Turn the power off, and nobody tops them up: everything is lost.

Why the cache is small: cache is SRAM. An SRAM cell is much bigger than a DRAM cell, so it has a lower bit density and costs more per bit. Making all the memory SRAM would cost far more and need far more chip space.

The ROM family: PROM, EPROM, EEPROM

About 6 minutes
The story

Every laptop warns you: do not switch off during a BIOS update. In the 1980s, arcade machines and early PCs kept their programs on EPROM chips with a little clear quartz window on top, covered by a sticker. Peel the sticker off and leave the chip in sunlight long enough, and the program could start to fade.

Check: which chip?

Show me step by step
  • PROM: Programmed once. Then it is fixed.
  • EPROM: Erasable with UV light. Take the chip out; the whole chip is wiped.
  • EEPROM: Electrically Erasable. It stays in the device, and you can change just part of it.

8

Embedded systems: where the firmware runs

About 5 minutes

Check: drawback or benefit?

What to learn

Drawbacks come up most often in questions: learn all four. Know the benefits too: small and low power, cheap to mass-produce, reliable because it does one job, starts instantly and responds in real time.

Show me step by step

Count the embedded systems you have used since you woke up: alarm clock, kettle, microwave, card reader on the bus, the school door entry system, a car. Each one does one job, and you cannot easily change what it does. The drawbacks all come from that.
